# RICE: Breaking Through the Training Bottlenecks of Reinforcement Learning with Explanation

## Overview
RICE proposes a two-stage refinement framework for sub-optimal DRL agents: (1) a simplified StateMask explanation module identifies critical states (steps most contributing to episode reward) in trajectories generated by a pre-trained policy; (2) these critical states are mixed with default initial states (mixing ratio p) to form an expanded initial distribution, from which a PPO agent refines the policy using Random Network Distillation (RND) exploration bonuses (weight λ).

The key theoretical result (Theorem 3.6) bounds the sub-optimality gap V^π*(ρ) − V^π′(ρ) ≤ O(ε / (1−γ)² · ‖dπ*/dπ̂ρ‖∞), and shows that the MaskNet-based sampling induces a better policy ˆπ (Lemma 3.5), thus tightening the bound compared to random state selection. The optimized StateMask replaces the primal-dual objective with J(θ) = max η(π̄) plus a blinding bonus R′ = R + α·am_t, enabling vanilla PPO training of the mask network.
