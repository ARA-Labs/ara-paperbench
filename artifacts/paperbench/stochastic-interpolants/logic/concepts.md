# Concepts

## Stochastic Interpolant
- **Notation**: $I_t = \alpha_t x_0 + \beta_t x_1 + \gamma_t z$, $t \in [0,1]$
- **Definition**: A continuous-time stochastic process parameterized by differentiable coefficient functions $\alpha_t, \beta_t, \gamma_t$ satisfying boundary conditions $\alpha_0 = \beta_1 = 1$, $\alpha_1 = \beta_0 = \gamma_0 = \gamma_1 = 0$, and $\alpha_t^2 + \beta_t^2 + \gamma_t^2 > 0$ for all $t \in [0,1]$. The pair $(x_0, x_1)$ is jointly drawn from $\rho(x_0, x_1)$, and $z \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I_d)$ independent of $(x_0, x_1)$.
- **Boundary conditions**: Applies when $\rho(x_0, x_1)$ has the correct marginals: $\int \rho(x_0, x_1)dx_1 = \rho_0(x_0)$ and $\int \rho(x_0, x_1)dx_0 = \rho_1(x_1)$. Fails if marginal conditions are violated.
- **Related concepts**: Data-dependent coupling, Velocity field, Transport equation

## Data-Dependent Coupling
- **Notation**: $\rho(x_0, x_1) = \rho_1(x_1)\rho_0(x_0|x_1)$
- **Definition**: A joint density over base sample $x_0$ and target sample $x_1$ in which the base sample is drawn conditionally given the target: $x_0 \sim \rho_0(\cdot|x_1)$. In practice, this is instantiated as $x_0 = m(x_1) + \sigma\zeta$ with $\zeta \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I_d)$, where $m(x_1)$ is a task-specific degradation of the target.
- **Boundary conditions**: Requires that $\int \rho_0(x_0|x_1)\rho_1(x_1)dx_1 = \rho_0(x_0)$ (marginal consistency). Contrast with independent coupling $\rho(x_0,x_1) = \rho_0(x_0)\rho_1(x_1)$.
- **Related concepts**: Stochastic interpolant, Inpainting coupling, Super-resolution coupling, Transport cost bound

## Velocity Field
- **Notation**: $b_t(x) = \mathbb{E}(\dot{I}_t | I_t = x)$
- **Definition**: The conditional expectation of the time-derivative of the stochastic interpolant, given the interpolant value. It defines the drift in the probability flow ODE $\dot{X}_t = b_t(X_t)$ whose solutions push $\rho_0$ forward to $\rho_1$.
- **Boundary conditions**: Uniquely minimizes the quadratic objective $\mathcal{L}_b(\hat{b}) = \int \mathbb{E}[|\hat{b}_t(I_t)|^2 - 2\dot{I}_t \cdot \hat{b}_t(I_t)]dt$ for any coupling with correct marginals.
- **Related concepts**: Stochastic interpolant, Transport equation, Score function, Probability flow ODE

## Transport Equation
- **Notation**: $\partial_t \rho_t(x) + \nabla \cdot (b_t(x)\rho_t(x)) = 0$
- **Definition**: The continuity equation satisfied by the time-dependent density $\rho_t(x)$ induced by the stochastic interpolant $I_t$. The density $\rho_t(x)$ interpolates between $\rho_0$ at $t=0$ and $\rho_1$ at $t=1$.
- **Boundary conditions**: Holds for any coupling with the correct marginals. Generalizes to the conditional case with $\rho_t(x|\xi)$ and $b_t(x,\xi)$.
- **Related concepts**: Velocity field, Probability flow ODE, Score function

## Probability Flow ODE
- **Notation**: $\dot{X}_t = b_t(X_t)$, with $X_{t=0} = x_0 \sim \rho_0$
- **Definition**: The deterministic ODE whose solution maps samples from $\rho_0$ at $t=0$ to samples from $\rho_1$ at $t=1$, using the learned velocity field $b_t$. Can be solved forward ($\rho_0 \to \rho_1$) or backward ($\rho_1 \to \rho_0$).
- **Boundary conditions**: Requires an accurate approximation of $b_t$; numerical integration error may be controlled by solving the Dopri (Dormand-Prince) adaptive ODE solver. At sampling time, $x_0$ is drawn from the data-dependent base $\rho_0(\cdot|x_1)$.
- **Related concepts**: Velocity field, Transport equation, Forward/backward SDE

## Score Function
- **Notation**: $\nabla \log \rho_t(x) = -\gamma_t^{-1} g_t(x)$, where $g_t(x) = \mathbb{E}(z|I_t=x)$
- **Definition**: The gradient of the log-density of the interpolant at time $t$, expressible as minus the inverse noise level times the conditional expectation of the noise $z$. The function $g_t$ minimizes $\mathcal{L}_g(\hat{g}) = \int \mathbb{E}[|\hat{g}_t(I_t)|^2 - 2z \cdot \hat{g}_t(I_t)]dt$.
- **Boundary conditions**: Only defined when $\gamma_t \neq 0$. When $\gamma_t = 0$ (as in the inpainting and super-resolution experiments with the simplified interpolant), the score is not available from this formula.
- **Related concepts**: Velocity field, Forward SDE, Backward SDE

## Inpainting Coupling
- **Notation**: $x_0 = \xi \circ x_1 + (1-\xi) \circ \zeta$, $\zeta \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I_d)$
- **Definition**: The base sample construction for image inpainting. The binary mask $\xi \in \{0,1\}^{C \times W \times H}$ preserves the clean pixels of the target $x_1$ (known region) and fills masked pixels with independent Gaussian noise. The interpolant uses $\alpha_t = t$, $\beta_t = 1-t$ (reversed coefficients relative to standard).
- **Boundary conditions**: Mask is randomly generated by tiling image into 64 tiles, each included with probability $p=0.3$. Mask takes same value for all channels at each spatial location.
- **Related concepts**: Data-dependent coupling, Stochastic interpolant

## Super-Resolution Coupling
- **Notation**: $x_0 = \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{D}(x_1)) + \sigma\zeta$, $\zeta \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I_d)$, $\sigma > 0$
- **Definition**: The base sample construction for super-resolution. $\mathcal{D}: \mathbb{R}^{C \times W \times H} \to \mathbb{R}^{C \times W_\text{low} \times H_\text{low}}$ crops the image to low resolution; $\mathcal{U}$ upsamples back using nearest-neighbor interpolation. Small Gaussian noise $\sigma\zeta$ is added to ensure the base density is non-degenerate (full-rank covariance in the ambient space).
- **Boundary conditions**: Requires $\sigma > 0$ to avoid concentration on a lower-dimensional manifold. The conditioning variable is $\xi = \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{D}(x_1))$ (low-res image), appended to channel dimension.
- **Related concepts**: Data-dependent coupling, Transport cost bound

## Transport Cost Bound
- **Notation**: $\mathbb{E}_{x_0 \sim \rho_0}[|X_{t=1}(x_0) - x_0|^2] \leq \int_0^1 \mathbb{E}[|\dot{I}_t|^2]dt$
- **Definition**: An upper bound on the expected squared displacement of the generative ODE, controlled by the kinetic energy of the interpolant. For the super-resolution coupling with $\alpha_t=1-t$, $\beta_t=t$, $\gamma_t=0$: $\dot{I}_t = x_1 - x_0$, so the bound reduces to $\mathbb{E}[|x_1 - x_0|^2] = d\sigma^2$ (dependent) vs $2\mathbb{E}[|x_1|^2] + d\sigma^2$ (independent).
- **Boundary conditions**: Bound is tight when the flow is length-minimizing (optimal transport). Minimizing this bound is equivalent to finding the Wasserstein-2 optimal transport map.
- **Related concepts**: Data-dependent coupling, Probability flow ODE, Stochastic interpolant

## Conditioning Variable
- **Notation**: $\xi \in \mathcal{D}$ (class label or continuous embedding); velocity field $b_t(x, \xi)$
- **Definition**: An external, task-specific signal independent of the coupling construction. Can be a discrete class label (e.g., ImageNet class) or a continuous embedding (e.g., low-resolution image for super-resolution). The velocity field and score are conditioned on $\xi$, enabling class-conditional or task-conditional generation. This is **distinct** from the data-dependent coupling: conditioning alters the velocity field, while coupling alters the starting distribution.
- **Boundary conditions**: Must be available both at training and inference time. In inpainting, $\xi$ is the binary mask; in super-resolution, $\xi = \mathcal{U}(\mathcal{D}(x_1))$ (the low-res image).
- **Related concepts**: Inpainting coupling, Super-resolution coupling, Velocity field
