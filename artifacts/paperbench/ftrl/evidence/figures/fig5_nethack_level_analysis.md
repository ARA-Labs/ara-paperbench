# Figure 5: NetHack Level 4 and Sokoban Analysis

**Source**: Figure 5, §5
**Claims**: C01, C02, C03, C05

## Description
Average return from Level 4 (top panel) and Sokoban filled pits (bottom panel) throughout fine-tuning. Models evaluated on 200 game saves generated by AutoAscend (agent loaded at the state AutoAscend was in upon entering the level). Measured every 25M environment steps.

## Level 4 Performance (Top Panel)
- **X-axis**: Environment steps (25M intervals, evaluated every 25M steps)
- **Y-axis**: Average return from Level 4 (on top of AutoAscend score); averaged over 200 game saves

| Method | Trajectory | Final Relative Performance |
|--------|-----------|---------------------------|
| Pre-trained π* (frozen) | Stable horizontal baseline | Reference |
| Fine-tuning + KS | Starts at π* level; steadily increases | Surpasses π* by end of training |
| Fine-tuning + BC | Similar to KS trajectory | Also surpasses π* |
| Fine-tuning + EWC | Roughly maintains π* level with temporary boost | Roughly equivalent to π* |
| Vanilla fine-tuning | Drops below π*; stays below | Lower than π* |
| Training from scratch | Lowest throughout | Never reaches π* level |

## Sokoban Level Performance (Bottom Panel)
- **X-axis**: Environment steps
- **Y-axis**: Average number of filled pits (Sokoban score); averaged over 200 game saves

| Method | Initial | During Training | Final | Notes |
|--------|---------|----------------|-------|-------|
| Pre-trained π* (frozen) | ~60–70 pits | Stable | ~60–70 pits | Baseline capability |
| Fine-tuning + BC | ~60–70 pits | Maintained | ~60–70 pits (matches π*) | BC uses expert data covering Sokoban |
| Fine-tuning + KS | ~60–70 pits | Drops to ~0 early | Minor recovery | Online data doesn't include Sokoban early |
| Fine-tuning + EWC | ~60–70 pits | Drops | Lower than π* | Never recovers to π* level |
| Vanilla fine-tuning | ~60–70 pits | ~0 | ~0 | Complete forgetting |
| Training from scratch | ~0 | ~0 | ~0 | Never learns Sokoban |

**Key insight**: KS struggles with Sokoban (deep dungeon level, online policy doesn't visit early); BC maintains it using expert data from entire dungeon.
